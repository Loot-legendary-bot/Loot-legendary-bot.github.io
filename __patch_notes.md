*Player-facing. Written by the CLASSEUR, newest first. The screen shows this file when you click the version number on the HOME menu.*

## 28 October 2026
- The tutorial (TUTO 1) now plays end to end by clicks, the portal included.
- New dice skin: **Silver Hallmark** — polished silver, and the LICK FLOOR tongue on the 1.
- **Pavise** now works: in combat you are immune to MELEE and RANGED damage until you use a Skill.
- **Casino**: when a card changes "a die" on a two-dice roll, you choose which die.
- **HI-SCORE**: quests now chain — every quest you complete is replaced, so you always have two to chase. No portal in HI-SCORE: survive and chain as long as you can.
- **VIP ROOM**: open to grade 30 and above.
- Bots got smarter: they heal when they can, don't charge a boss they can't kill, and keep their Troll Yogurt, Troll Milk and Burrata di Troll for bosses.
- French: all character abilities are now translated (stats stay STR / DEX / WIS / AGI / HP).
- **The Poisoner** now unlocks at **50 natural 20s** (was 150).
