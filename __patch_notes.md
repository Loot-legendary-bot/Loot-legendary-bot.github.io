# LICK FLOOR — Patch notes
*Player-facing, condensed, newest first. **From 29 Oct 2026 20:30 UTC, written by the SCREEN at every publication** (it knows what goes online and when) — the CLASSEUR only checks it. Times are UTC (GMT+0), the moment the change was pushed. The screen shows this file when you click the version number at the bottom right of the HOME menu.*

## 07 Nov 2026
- **13:41** — BOUNTY WALL is now the star map. Clicking a spirit on the HOME wall opens the map and glides to that spirit's branch (the BOUNTY WALL label opens the whole star); BACK or Esc takes you home. A bounty whose reward is waiting for you glows gold with READY; its card has a **CLAIM IT ON THE WALL ▸** button. Pacts, offerings, rewards to claim and contracts are one click away with **PACTS & OFFERINGS ▸** (the wall page you know, which reopens the map with ✶ THE STAR).
- **13:35** — BOUNTY WALL star map: you can now travel it. Scroll or pinch to zoom smoothly from the whole star down to a single bounty filling the screen; drag to move; double-click a bounty or a spirit to fly to it; double-click an empty spot or press ⤢ OVERVIEW to see the whole star again. Up close, every bounty also writes its condition under its reward, and clicking one opens its card: spirit, state, the condition in a full sentence ("Kill yourself once.") and your progress. Esc closes the card, then the map.
- **13:25** — BOUNTY WALL: first look at the star map. On the Bounty Wall page, the new **✶ THE STAR** button (under SEE ALL) opens the whole wall as one parchment map: the Spirit of the Dungeon in the middle with its bounties on two rings, the six school spirits at the six points of the star, and each spirit's bounties around it — done ones lit with a ✓, the others with their counter and progress ring, a sleeping spirit's branch greyed and dotted; each bounty's reward is written beside it, and your pact spirit wears a gold ring. Esc or BACK closes it. Zoom and moving around come next.
- **13:14** — Big hits: every hit of 20 damage or more now takes the whole screen for a second — the number huge, in the colour of the hit's school (fire orange, frost blue…), the name of whoever took it below, and a light screen shake. Smaller hits keep their floating number on the target, now bigger and longer the harder the hit, also in the school's colour. With VFX on OFF, none of this plays; with reduced motion, no shake.
- **12:59** — Watching someone else's fight: the middle of the screen now shows each fighter's dice tray, big, with every roll landing there, and below it the row of cards played in this fight, face up and in order (they stay; before, a played card vanished after 3 seconds). The fight's log stays on the side.
- **12:48** — Dice tray: its title strip is now an arcade-style LED display. While a die rolls it shows the roll (MARVELOUS CHEST · 4D20, THE COLLAPSE · 1D20…); a few seconds after it lands, it slowly scrolls the table's news — run and turn, next Collapse die, next portal chance, and your open quest — then comes back to the roll, over and over. A portal roll glows blue.
- **12:27** — The Tamarin (Tiny) at the Collapse: before Dungeon Turn 11 his die is now a PORTAL ROLL — the tray titles it **THE PORTAL ROLL** with its die (1D6, 1D8…), he takes no damage and is asked nothing (unless he holds a card that changes a die), and if the die opens the portal his tray writes **PORTAL OPEN ON THIS TILE** (before, his die could never open a portal). Dice tray: the 11:47 spread of several dice is online with this update. Rules engine merged (crossbows: one Weapon Attack per fight per wielder).
- **11:47** — Dice tray: several dice (a 4d20 chest, a 3d20) are now spread across the whole tray, none on top of another; a single die still lands anywhere.
- **11:33** — Dice tray: when your Collapse die opens the portal on your Tile, the tray writes **PORTAL OPEN ON THIS TILE** under the die once it lands (also in another player's tray). Exploration: the d6 plays the recorded dice roll again, and the chest sound only plays on a chest, when the die lands. The game opens again after today's Daily calendar data arrived (it stopped on a black screen in the local build).
- **01:12** — Character select: The Boltwright's square shows his portrait again (it was an empty dashed frame, his animated selection art doesn't exist yet).
- **00:54** — Dice tray: when a card changes one die of a roll (Dungeon Clover on the Offering Pit, "which die?"), the tray now shows the new face — the changed die is ringed in gold with its old face above it ("3 →"); before, the tray kept the dice as first rolled. Rogue's Shortcut and Bone: once played, a compass badge "+1" on your sheet (under your HP) shows the charge waiting for your next Exploration; when you explore, the tray writes the bonus under the die ("3 +1 = 4") — the die shows what you rolled, the Tile reads the total. Rules engine merged (The Mosquito now counts a Banana Flee).

- **12:00** — The dungeon turn line (RUN · TURN · next Collapse · next portal) now hits harder: when a new Dungeon Turn begins it swells and glows gold for a second, and when the Collapse falls it flares again in ember orange, then settles back to its quiet size.
- **10:04** — CODEX: loot cards are now listed by rarity (Common → Legendary), then by name; the characters' base weapons have their own BASE WEAPONS section at the end, always shown face up with their art (Morning Star and Rotten Salmon included) and the characters who start with each one.

- **08:02** — Shared dice screens: at the Collapse, every player's die now rolls at the same time before landing on its face (sometimes only one of them used to spin); the start-of-game roll (who goes first), the highest-roll and shared-roll scenes now roll all their dice too, instead of showing them already landed. Rules engine: a player who will come back (respawn) no longer ends the run.
