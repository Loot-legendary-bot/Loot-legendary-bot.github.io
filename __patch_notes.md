# LICK FLOOR — Patch notes
*Player-facing, condensed, newest first. **From 29 Oct 2026 20:30 UTC, written by the SCREEN at every publication** (it knows what goes online and when) — the CLASSEUR only checks it. Times are UTC (GMT+0), the moment the change was pushed. The screen shows this file when you click the version number at the bottom right of the HOME menu.*

## 01 Nov 2026
- **12:16** — Engine: a fight started during the Upkeep now waits for the whole table's Collapse — no more Collapse rolling in the middle of a fight. Hovering a line of your character sheet zooms it again: a clear framed box, 1.6× bigger, next to your cursor, never cut off. Five new card backs in the Vault (500 teeth): Goblin Casino, Stitched, Nylon Wallet, Dark Tapestry, Green Black Hype. END TURN now sits in your hand box, right next to your hand and Signature, and the countdown is written on the button ("ENDS IN 12 s"); the dice tray takes the freed space and is taller. The Golmon's Primal Path no longer prints a code in the middle of the screen: "TILE REDRAW · THE GOLMON PAYS 3 HP", the tile sent back and the new one. Cards that destroy or steal someone's stuff (Devouring Moths, Listener Scarab…) are seen by everyone: "X PLAYS DEVOURING MOTHS on Y", and the card flies from X to Y. Redirections (Mirror of Reversal…) too: "↪ X REDIRECTS <card> · from Y → Z", the card flying from the old target to the new one. Before your die is rolled, when you hold Gorma Juice or Dungeon Clover, the window says "A DIE IS ABOUT TO BE ROLLED" and its button is ROLL THE DIE; the card you put down lies on the felt of your dice tray next to the die, with its bonus (+2).
- **10:32** — The Wizard's school window says "(recommended)" under Fire, Frost and Arcane. Watching a fight you are not in: you now see exactly the screen of the fighter you follow — that player's sheet on the left, the other fighters on the right in tabs (one tab per fighter, like when you fight yourself), what happens in this fight and your bet in the middle. No more two cards face to face with VS, whatever the number of fighters, and no "YOUR TARGET" mark when you only watch. Also in this update: Doom Book's redirection now really sends the blow back to its bearer, and the Ring of Antimagic is no longer offered against a blow that does not use WIS.
- **03:17** — Dice tray: several dice (2d10, 3d20, the 4d20 of a big chest) are now nearly as big as a single d6 — up to 1.4× bigger than before — still scattered on the felt without touching.
- **02:59** — Dice: when your dice roll in your tray, nothing else happens until they land — the window asking you to react, the result line and the HP change come after (also when you watch another player). Combat: every line of your character sheet carries a symbol — ▶ what you can click (your skills, Flee, your Signature), ◇ what triggers on its own (your Reaction); click your sheet (or ⤢ in its corner) to open it whole and large in the middle of the screen, Esc or a click outside to close (the small zoom on a single line is gone). A hit on a monster now names it ("6 DMG → ASTRAGOR", no more "→ ?").
- **01:20** — Dice: every die now looks the same size as the d6 (d4, d8, d10, d12 and d20 were drawn smaller), alone or several at once, in your dice tray and in the middle of the screen.
- **01:02** — The Dining Room is a still picture again (the animated test is switched off: it was unstable and pixelated). Your Signature card stays lit from the start of the game: it only turns grey once you have used it this Dungeon Turn; when it cannot be played right now it keeps its colours and says why at its foot.

## 31 Oct 2026
- **21:28** — Online: when you are asked, OK · CONTINUE always passes now, and at 0 s the game passes for you — even after a lost connection or a move the server refused (before, every later pass of that seat was silently dropped). When time runs out on a question the game does not answer for you (discarding down to your hand limit), the screen says so and waits for your cards instead of claiming "PASS applied". Character select: BACK TO MENU asks first — "Leave the table?" STAY / LEAVE. Dice rolled by a card or a room are named ("The Squire Spa · Purifying Bath · heal 2d10"), not "an op's die argument". Troll Milk, Troll Yogurt and the third Troll now add up.
- **19:05** — Traps and chests now play in order: first the card alone in the middle, then your dice roll in your dice tray, then the result (the damage you take, or the rarities you draw). The result no longer shows before the card.
- **16:58** — Character select: the portraits are full squares, and you confirm with a big CONFIRM CHARACTER plate (the game's painted button), BACK beside it.
- **15:35** — The Wizard picks his school in a real window: the six schools as big tiles, one click to choose, your choice written below, then OK (no more drop-down list).
- **14:38** — Before run 2 starts, a READY box: "RUN 2 — READY?", how many players are ready, a READY button and the timer; nothing of run 2 (draw, Collapse) happens before everyone is ready.
- **14:22** — New dice in the Vault: White Paint dice (matte black, thick white brushed numbers, one edge dipped in blood). Mirror of Reversal and the other cards that redirect or cancel a card now name the opponent's card even after you drank a potion first.
- **13:28** — When a card can redirect or cancel another card (Mirror of Reversal, Le Croissant…), it now says which one: "↪ REDIRECTS Devouring Moths (The Mailman)", with a small picture of that card — on the card in your hand and in the reaction window.
- **13:02** — The Dining Room's animated room: no more black bars at the top and bottom, its colours match the still picture, and the loop no longer jumps when it starts over.
- **12:07** — Dice tray: your dice now roll in your tray, not in the middle of the screen (rolls of the whole table, like the Collapse, still play in the middle and then land in the tray already rolled). Each roll plays once, one smooth turn — no more double spin. Coming back to a finished fight, another player's page or a reloaded game: the dice are already lying on their faces. Out of combat the dice are 30% smaller.
- **11:40** — The Dining Room is now animated: when you stand on it, the room behind the table moves (the fire burns), in a silent loop. The other rooms are unchanged. It is a test before animating every room.
- **10:30** — A mob that strikes first no longer hits from an empty table: after its card is revealed, the fight (COMBAT, you vs the mob, its card as YOUR TARGET) is on screen before its hit, and your HP and the log line come after the hit.
- **09:55** — Watching a fight of four or more players: you see the screen of the fighter you follow (their sheet on the left, the other fighters on the right, the log and the bets in the middle); up to three, the VS columns stay. The Collapse die no longer rolls a second time in your dice tray after the table roll. The rarities in the dice tray use the same colours as the run banner. Gorma Juice and Clover can now be played before the die is thrown on an ability roll, a weapon's damage dice, the exploration d6, the exploration heal d20 and the coffin d20: the bonus is added and the dice roll once. After your Signature on a hit, your Reaction is offered again. A card that moves you out before a hit lands makes it miss you. Prismatic Ring works on any die, in combat too. Ring of Lava deals 5 Fire to the other players of your tile when you enter it or someone enters yours. Sala Septa no longer adds one Fire too many: it deals what its card says.
